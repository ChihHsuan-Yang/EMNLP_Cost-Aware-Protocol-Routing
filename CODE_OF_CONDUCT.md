# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the research community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, and discussions, and when an individual is representing the project
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the project maintainers at **bellayang@anl.gov**. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
